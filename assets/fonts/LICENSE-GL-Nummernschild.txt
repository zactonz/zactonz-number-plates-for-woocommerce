Why this font is redistributed with the plugin
----------------------------------------------

A German plate has to be set in FE-Schrift; the typeface is part of the specification,
not a styling choice. An all-permissive grant of the kind quoted above is what makes
redistribution possible, and this is the only plate face found under one. Faces for
other countries, and FE-Engschrift for the longest German registrations, are not
bundled for exactly that reason - they are added by the merchant through the
zctz_np_profile_faces filter, from a file they are licensed to use.

The grant above permits commercial use, modification and redistribution without
condition, which is compatible with this plugin's GPL-2.0-or-later licence. The font
remains the copyright of Gutenberg Labo and is not covered by that licence.
